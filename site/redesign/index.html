02Introduction

Retrieval systems answer.

They rarely say how well-founded the answer is.

Autognose keeps what a system knows about its own knowledge: where a piece came from, whether it was ever checked, whether it still holds, and how confident the system is entitled to be. Its job is to decide, not to generate.

03Thesis

What we
believe.

T1

Meta-knowledge first.

Every item carries its own provenance and status; nothing is built on items that lack them.

T2

Evaluated, or it does not count.

If we cannot measure whether a system declines when it should, the capability is unbuilt.

04Architecture

Assessment in,
decision out.

Autognose is a library, not a retriever. It sits beside your retrieval stack, maintains a model of its own knowledge state, and hands back a decision. Your agent stays in control of its own loop.

Decision, not generation

Given a question and what was retrieved: answer, retrieve more, ask, or decline. A decision made honestly is what we mean by self-aware.

Thick on state, thin on plumbing

Recorded ruling: Autognose owns knowledge state and builds no retriever. The caller decides whether to act on the assessment.

Schema: open on purpose

The knowledge-state field set is the project's longest-standing open decision — it determines the public API, so none exists yet.

06Applications

Where this
earns its keep.

The wedge is small on purpose: assess evidence state, change the action. Two settings where that already matters.

A·01

RAG augmentation

Your pipeline retrieves; Autognose assesses. Thin evidence, conflicting passages, superseded sources — surfaced before the answer ships.

A·02

Knowledge management

Corporate knowledge bases that know what is stale. Outdated policy documents get flagged by the system that serves them.

A·03

Honest abstention

A system that declines when the evidence is thin — and can show you why — instead of answering confidently anyway.
