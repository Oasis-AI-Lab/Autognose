Autognose

自知 · Know what you don't. Act accordingly.

Autognose maintains what a system knows about its own knowledge — where a piece came from, whether it was ever checked, whether it still holds — and turns it into a decision: answer, retrieve more, ask, or abstain.

02 System overview

Assessment in.
Decision out.

Autognose is planned as a library, not a retriever — a recorded decision. It sits beside your retrieval stack and hands back one decision per question. The diagram shows the intended relationships; dashed elements are conceptual and open. Click any node.

Recorded / planned interface Conceptual · open boundary Evaluated first

Read me

Select a node

Each node carries its decision status — recorded, proposed, or open. Nothing on this diagram is implemented; the distinction between recorded and proposed is the honest part.

Decision, not generation

Given a question and what was retrieved, the output is a decision made honestly. A decision you can audit is what "self-aware" means here — nothing more, nothing mystical.

Thick on state, thin on plumbing

Recorded ruling: own the knowledge state, build no retriever, deliver as a library. The caller stays in control of its own loop.

Evaluated or it does not count

No capability claim without a harness that could fail. Baselines ship with every comparison, and degenerate systems must score badly.
